## Question

What are the universal invariants of good project documentation — the things that hold true regardless of domain, scale, or medium? Candidatess surfaced so far: docs rot, audience-awareness, findability, navigability, minimality, cost-of-entry, being versioned/time-aware, evolving via knowledge merging, and teaching through examples. Validate, refine, add, and remove from this list. Capture each invariant with its rationale, a concrete example of violation, and a concrete example of adherence.
