## Question

Research documentation practices and lessons learned from high-stakes environments (military, critical infrastructure, aerospace, healthcare) and passionate communities (open-source projects with excellent docs, hobbyist communities, knowledge-sharing collectives). The hypothesis: the best documentation comes from environments where failure is expensive or where contributors care deeply. Corporate documentation tends to be garbage. Capture patterns, anti-patterns, structural approaches, conventions, maintenance procedures, and any documentation-operations wisdom that the `documentation` skill should incorporate.
